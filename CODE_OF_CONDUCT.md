# Code of conduct

Dreadcast follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be welcoming and considerate, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the
work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks aren't tolerated anywhere in the project:
issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent it.

## Reporting

Email **kevin@dreadcast.app** about anything that breaks this code, including in private
messages. Reports are read by the maintainer, kept confidential, and answered. If a
report concerns the maintainer, say so, and it will be handled with that in mind.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that don't follow this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or
permanently, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.

Security problems go through private vulnerability reporting instead; see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
